Why week 4 differs

My Week 4 diagram had five entities (Department, Student, Lecturer, Course and Enrolment), but the reverse-engineered diagram has six tables, because the Course–Lecturer many-to-many relationship across sessions needed its own junction table, CourseAllocation, alongside Enrolment for Student–Course.
The Week 4 diagram was a design sketch with loosely defined attributes, while the Workbench diagram comes from the real tables and so shows exact data types, AUTO_INCREMENT keys, UNIQUE and NOT NULL constraints, and DEFAULT values, plus columns added during implementation such as phone, credit_units, academic_session, semester and enrolled_on.
In the reverse-engineered diagram, every relationship is drawn from an actual FOREIGN KEY constraint as a one-to-many line into the parent table, and the junction tables are identified by their composite UNIQUE keys (for example student_id, course_id, academic_session), where my Week 4 diagram only showed the relationships conceptually.
